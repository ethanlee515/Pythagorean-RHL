\section{Conclusion and Future Works}
\label{sec:limitations}

Our formalization deliberately factors correctness failures from the noise-flooding argument.
For an imperfectly correct FHE such as CKKS,
one would add an additional game hop (i.e. consider a hybrid experiment where correctness holds with probability one),
and apply a standard union bound.

The chart interface from \Cref{eq:charts} also remains abstract.
A concrete CKKS instantiation must connect its plaintext ring and norm to those
centering, distance, displacement-bound, and translation assumptions.

Modulo the above proof engineering items, we have formally verified the central LMSS game hop \cite{lmss}.
Under the assumptions collected in \Cref{sec:assumptions}, noise flooding
reduces the IND-CPAD winning probability to the supplied IND-CPA bound plus
the loss \(\sqrt{qn}/(2\gamma)\).
The natural next step then, is to verify that the CKKS scheme is indeed both IND-CPA and approximately correct.
That will certainly come with its own set of other interesting challenges.
For instance, such an effort will no doubt contend with the use of randomized versus deterministic rounding, as well as the (difficult to formalize) heuristics involved in the deterministic case as found ``in the wilds".
